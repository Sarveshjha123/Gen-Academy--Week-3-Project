# Master Supply Agreement: Brightline Motion

> Fictional document created for a course project. Northfield Industrial and Brightline Motion do not exist.

This Master Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northfield Industrial, Inc. ("Buyer") and Brightline Motion, with offices in Dayton, OH, USA ("Supplier"). Agreement number MSA-V02-2025.

## 1. Term and Renewal
This Agreement takes effect on May 30, 2025 and remains in force until April 21, 2028 (the "Expiry Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 10. This Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Products and Contract Prices
Supplier will sell the products listed in Appendix A at the contract unit prices shown there. Contract prices include standard packaging. Prices exclude sales tax and the per-order freight charge stated in Appendix A. Any quotation issued by Supplier at a price above the Appendix A price is not valid for products covered by this Agreement unless Buyer's Procurement Head accepts it in writing.

## 3. Price Adjustments
Supplier may request a price adjustment no more than once in any twelve-month period, with sixty (60) days' written notice. Any increase shall not exceed 4% in a twelve-month period and must be supported by documented changes in raw-material or labor costs.

## 4. Orders, Minimum Quantities and Lead Times
Buyer places Purchase Orders ("POs") from time to time. Each PO must meet the minimum order quantity ("MOQ") in Appendix A. Supplier confirms each PO within two (2) business days and commits to a Promised Delivery Date no later than the standard lead time shown in Appendix A.

## 5. Delivery, Fulfilment and Late Delivery
5.1 Fill rate. Supplier shall fulfil at least 97% of ordered units complete and on time, measured each calendar quarter.
5.2 Late delivery. If any delivery arrives after the Promised Delivery Date plus three (3) days, Supplier will credit Buyer 2% of the late shipment's invoice value per week_or_part (or part thereof counted as full) of delay, capped at 12% of the affected Purchase Order. Liquidated damages are Buyer's sole monetary remedy for late delivery, without prejudice to termination rights for repeated failure.
5.3 Partial shipments require Buyer's prior consent.

## 6. Inspection, Damage and Defects
Buyer will inspect goods on receipt. Buyer must notify Supplier of visible damage, shortage or defects within seven (7) days of receipt. For goods damaged in transit or found defective, Supplier's remedy is full credit or replacement at buyer's choice; replacements ship within fifteen (15) days of the claim. Return freight for damaged or defective goods is paid by the vendor. Claims notified after the claim window are accepted only at Supplier's discretion.

## 7. Warranty
Supplier warrants that products are free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months from delivery and conform to the agreed specifications.

## 8. Invoicing and Payment
Supplier invoices on shipment. Payment terms are Net 30 from the date a correct invoice is received. Invoices that do not match the PO price are held until corrected.

## 9. Insurance and Liability
Supplier maintains general liability insurance of at least USD 2,000,000 per occurrence. Except for injury, fraud or confidentiality breaches, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to USD 1,000,000.

## 10. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with sixty (60) days' written notice. Buyer may terminate immediately if Supplier misses the fill-rate commitment in Section 5.1 for two consecutive quarters.

## 11. Force Majeure
Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party within five (5) days and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. Force majeure does not excuse payment obligations.

## 12. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties for its subject matter and may be changed only in a signed writing.
