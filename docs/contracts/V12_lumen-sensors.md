# Master Supply Agreement: Lumen Sensors

> Fictional document created for a course project. Northfield Industrial and Lumen Sensors do not exist.

This Master Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northfield Industrial, Inc. ("Buyer") and Lumen Sensors, with offices in Hsinchu, Taiwan ("Supplier"). Agreement number MSA-V12-2024.

## 1. Term and Renewal
This Agreement takes effect on May 25, 2024 and remains in force until March 15, 2027 (the "Expiry Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 10. This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any extension must be agreed in a signed written amendment.

## 2. Products and Contract Prices
Supplier will sell the products listed in Appendix A at the contract unit prices shown there. Contract prices include standard packaging. Prices exclude sales tax and the per-order freight charge stated in Appendix A. Any quotation issued by Supplier at a price above the Appendix A price is not valid for products covered by this Agreement unless Buyer's Procurement Head accepts it in writing.

## 3. Price Adjustments
Supplier may request a price adjustment no more than once in any twelve-month period, with sixty (60) days' written notice. Any increase shall not exceed 6% in a twelve-month period and must be supported by documented changes in raw-material or labor costs.

## 4. Orders, Minimum Quantities and Lead Times
Buyer places Purchase Orders ("POs") from time to time. Each PO must meet the minimum order quantity ("MOQ") in Appendix A. Supplier confirms each PO within two (2) business days and commits to a Promised Delivery Date no later than the standard lead time shown in Appendix A.

## 5. Delivery, Fulfilment and Late Delivery
5.1 Fill rate. Supplier shall fulfil at least 99% of ordered units complete and on time, measured each calendar quarter.
5.2 Late delivery. Supplier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet Promised Delivery Dates. No liquidated damages, credits or penalties apply to late delivery under this Agreement. Buyer's remedy for persistent lateness is limited to termination under Section 10.
5.3 Partial shipments require Buyer's prior consent.

## 6. Inspection, Damage and Defects
Buyer will inspect goods on receipt. Buyer must notify Supplier of visible damage, shortage or defects within seven (7) days of receipt. For goods damaged in transit or found defective, Supplier's remedy is replace at vendor cost; replacements ship within five (5) days of the claim. Return freight for damaged or defective goods is paid by the buyer. Claims notified after the claim window are accepted only at Supplier's discretion.

## 7. Warranty
Supplier warrants that products are free from defects in materials and workmanship for twenty-four (24) months from delivery and conform to the agreed specifications.

## 8. Invoicing and Payment
Supplier invoices on shipment. Payment terms are Net 45 from the date a correct invoice is received. Invoices that do not match the PO price are held until corrected.

## 9. Insurance and Liability
Supplier maintains general liability insurance of at least USD 2,000,000 per occurrence. Except for injury, fraud or confidentiality breaches, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to 2x annual contract value.

## 10. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with sixty (60) days' written notice. Buyer may terminate immediately if Supplier misses the fill-rate commitment in Section 5.1 for two consecutive quarters.

## 11. Force Majeure
Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party within five (5) days and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. Force majeure does not excuse payment obligations.

## 12. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. It is the entire agreement between the parties for its subject matter and may be changed only in a signed writing.
